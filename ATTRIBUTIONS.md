| Publication | Date | URL |
|---|---|---|
| 《口腔类医疗服务价格项目立项指南（试行）》 | 2025-03-03 | https://www.nhsa.gov.cn/art/2025/3/3/art_201_15851.html |
| 《口腔种植类医疗服务价格项目立项指南（试行）》 | 2024-11-18 | https://www.nhsa.gov.cn/art/2024/11/18/art_201_14728.html |
| 国家医保局印发《口腔类医疗服务价格项目立项指南（试行）》通知 | 2025-02-28 | https://www.nhsa.gov.cn/art/2025/2/28/art_52_15815.html |

**Note**: NHSA 立项指南 defines project identities, billing units, and surcharge/extension logic. **Actual prices are set by provincial 医保局 (Provincial Healthcare Security Administrations)** and are NOT included in this pilot.

NHSA 立项指南只定项目边界与计价规则；**实际价格由省级医保局制定**，本试点不含价格。

## Tooth anatomy / 根管解剖

Canal-count alerts (MB2, C-shape, etc.) are based on:

- Vertucci FJ. *Root canal anatomy of the human permanent teeth.* Oral Surg Oral Med Oral Pathol 1984;58(5):589-599.
- Cleghorn BM, Christie WH, Dong CCS. *Root and root canal morphology of the human permanent maxillary first molar: a literature review.* J Endod 2006;32(9):813-821.
- Cohen's Pathways of the Pulp, Ch. 7 (Root and Pulp Anatomy)
- Chinese-population C-shape canal prevalence: various Chinese-language dental journals (合并引用，未逐条核对)
